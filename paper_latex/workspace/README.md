# Peer Agreement as a Gold-Free Signal of NL-to-FOL Faithfulness, and Where It Fails

LaTeX source and compiled PDF of the paper. The paper asks whether agreement among
several LLMs' first-order-logic formalizations of the same sentence, checked by Z3 under a
name-free vocabulary alignment, predicts whether a formalization is faithful to its sentence.
On 450 fresh sentences from nine LLMs, a reliability-weighted peer agreement score reaches
0.827 AUROC under LLM-panel labels and 0.874 under audited-solver labels, against 0.791 and 0.727
for a gemini-2.5-flash judge. The evidence is preliminary: the score adds about 0.03 AUROC over
a judge-plus-round-trip baseline (95% CI [-0.001, 0.066]), ties binary Dawid-Skene agreement, and
falls below chance when most systems share an error.
